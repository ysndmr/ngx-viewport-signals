02 — viewportRatio()

How much of this panel do you actually see?

A continuous 0–1 signal of how much of this element intersects the viewport — handy for scroll-linked progress indicators, "read progress" bars, or fading effects tied to visibility.

{{ ratio() | percent: '1.0-0' }}
const ratio = viewportRatio(this.panelRef);
// ratio() -> 0..1, updates continuously on scroll
